# CircleOpenSearch
- CircleOpenSearch is an open-source alternative to Circle-to-Search. It has Google Lens, Color Picker, and Shazam (to identify music playing on your device or around you). And of course, you can circle something on your screen to search for it
- Made with AI
- It works as an assistant, so you can set it to launch with a long press of the power button. I'll probably add shortcuts in the future, so you can run it through Key Mapper, for example

### Why not Circle to Search?
I don't like this white line at the bottom of the phone (I can run Color Picker only by holding it). And I also wanted a color picker

### How to use it?
- Tap the middle button to send the entire screenshot to Google Lens
- Tap the left button to identify a song playing on your device
- Hold the left button to identify a song playing around you
- Tap the right button to enter color picker mode
- In color picker mode, tap the big circle to copy the color
- Circle an area on the screenshot to search for it in Google Lens

To activate CircleOpenSearch as your assistant, go to Settings -> Apps -> Default apps -> Digital assistant app -> Select Circle Open Search
